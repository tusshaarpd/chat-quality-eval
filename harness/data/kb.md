# Contoso Workplace Handbook (excerpt) — internal reference documents

## [DOC-1] Leave policy (effective 2026-04-01)
- Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month.
- Up to 8 unused annual-leave days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.
- Sick leave: 12 days per year. A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive sick days.
- Parental leave: 26 weeks for the primary caregiver, 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver, both fully paid.
- Leave requests go through the "MyHR" portal and need manager approval at least 5 working days in advance (sick leave excepted).

## [DOC-2] Travel and expense policy
- Domestic flights: economy class only. International flights over 6 hours: premium economy allowed with VP approval.
- Hotel cap: INR 7,000 per night in Tier-1 Indian cities (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Pune); INR 4,500 elsewhere in India.
- Daily meal allowance: INR 1,500 domestic; USD 60 international.
- Expenses must be filed in the "Expensify" tool within 30 days of the trip end date. Late claims need finance director approval.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## [DOC-3] IT and security policy
- Passwords: minimum 14 characters, rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) via Microsoft Authenticator is mandatory.
- Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk (ext. 4357, or helpdesk@contoso.example) within 2 hours.
- Personal devices may access email only through the Outlook mobile app with Intune enrollment.
- Sharing customer data with external AI tools is prohibited. Approved internal assistant: "Contoso Copilot".
- VPN is required for access to internal finance systems.

## [DOC-4] Remote work policy
- Hybrid model: employees are expected in the office 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday are anchor days).
- Fully remote arrangements require director approval and are reviewed every 6 months.
- A one-time home-office stipend of INR 25,000 is available after 90 days of employment.
- Working from another country for more than 14 days in a year needs HR and tax approval.

## [DOC-5] Performance and promotion cycle
- Performance reviews happen twice a year: April (mid-year check-in) and October (annual review).
- Ratings scale: Exceeds, Meets, Partially Meets, Does Not Meet.
- Promotions are decided only in the October cycle. An employee must have been in their current level for at least 12 months.
- Annual merit increases are effective 1 January.
